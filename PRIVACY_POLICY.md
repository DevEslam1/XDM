# Privacy Policy for XDM

**Last Updated: August 23, 2026**

XDM ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **Downloaded Files & URLs:** All download links, file metadata, and downloaded content are stored locally on your device in your designated storage directories and local application database. We do not transmit your download links, history, or file contents to external proprietary servers.
- **Browser Cookies:** If you enable the built-in browser component, cookies and web session data remain in your local browser sandbox. Cookie forwarding is disabled by default.
- **Torrent Data:** BitTorrent peer exchanges and DHT interactions communicate directly with decentralized swarm peers for the purpose of downloading requested torrent pieces.

## 2. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics
- If error telemetry or crash reporting is enabled, anonymized diagnostic information (stack traces, operating system version, error messages) may be sent to our crash reporting system (e.g. Sentry) to help us resolve crashes and improve stability. Personally identifiable information (PII) such as passwords, auth tokens, and private paths is scrubbed before transmission.

## 3. Permissions
- **Storage & Media:** Required to save downloaded files to your device.
- **Notifications:** Used to display real-time download progress and completion notifications.
- **Network Access:** Required to connect to download sources and peer swarms.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The app may query public APIs or services explicitly requested by user actions (such as fetching stream metadata or update manifests). Such requests are subject to the privacy policies of those third-party providers.

## 5. Contact
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the official project repository.
